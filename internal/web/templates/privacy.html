Privacy Notice

Session cookie

Packmon uses the first-party packmon_session cookie for admin login sessions, login-form CSRF protection, and logout. The cookie is scoped to /admin, is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and expires with the configured admin session lifetime.

Operational metadata

The server can store admin audit log entries for security-sensitive actions, including action names, timestamps, client address metadata, and user-agent metadata. Scan history and scan metadata can contain package names, versions, ecosystems, repository metadata supplied by clients, result counts, and operational errors needed to troubleshoot dependency-security decisions.

Operator notice

Packmon is intended for internal deployment. Your organization controls retention, access, and any additional privacy or legal notices for its environment.
